# Front-facing Mario reference

`front-reference.png` is the sprite sheet supplied by the user on 2026-10-22. The sheet credits CursedMario for recreated/edited front-facing sprites from Game & Watch: Super Mario Bros.

The small Fire Mario at rectangle `(9, 61)` to `(25, 77)` is used pixel-for-pixel for the front-facing pose, with its background and three character colours mapped to NES palette indices. The Zelda palette uses bright white so Mario remains visible against the sand. The rear view, attack and item-lifting adaptations are created for this port, and the side poses come from the user's local SMB1 ROM.

The standing front pose is retained exactly. Vertical walking uses additional strides with an extended front leg, raised rear foot and opposite arm swing. The two step phases preserve the reference's symmetric head while alternating the lower body.
