MARIO PORT PHYSICS FIXES - 23 October 2026

Installed the verified updates in C:\nes. The original game ROMs and the
local SMB1base source are unchanged. Start each updated ROM from a fresh reset;
old emulator save states contain the previous code and private player state.

CHANGES

Contra: grounded Down+direction uses SMB1 input filtering. Walking off a
ledge retains the falling force chosen by the preceding jump. Native gun
controls, water/shore behavior, platform drops and room scripts remain.
Fireballs share an enemy collision-box calculation across a volley and refresh
it after damage changes native actor state.

Rescue Rangers: crouch jumps retain the short body throughout flight, and
grounded Down+direction uses SMB1 filtering. Native carrying, throws, box
hiding and Sparky hazards remain. Sprite rows reuse coordinates, native OAM
copies use a precomputed bound, and state transfers use unrolled copies in
private cartridge RAM. The full 64-sprite output and all stage palettes pass.

Kung Fu: restored SMB1 input/update order, speed-dependent jump launch,
neutral-air coasting, facing, retained airborne crouching, fractional movement,
gravity after position, and the source's fractional fall limit. From-rest tap
and held jump apexes are 24 and 64 pixels. Punches/kicks, native boss barriers,
deaths, entrances and staircase scripts remain and pass their replays.

Tetris: restored neutral-air momentum/facing, jump selection before horizontal
acceleration, fractional falling behavior and selected force on ledge falls.
Floor contact resolves at the feet during the landing update. Native Tetris
piece controls, modes, CPU play, safe head breaks and piece pushes/stomps pass.

SMB2 USA: normal player movement now uses signed position fractions and full
8.8 vertical velocity. Position advances using the old velocity before SMB1
gravity is applied. Native collision velocity changes are imported, while
moving supports add their displacement without changing persistent velocity.
Native scripted states and deep quicksand retain their native integrator.
Hearts, charged jumps, climbing, carrying, throws and room transitions pass.
Mario/Luigi from-rest tap/held jumps reproduce 24/64-pixel apexes.

Kid Icarus needed no ordinary land-physics change; its fresh source comparison
passes. Gradius's floating flight, Snake's isometric movement, Zelda's overhead
movement and WrestleMania's wrestling movement remain intentional adaptations.
Their ROM bytes are unchanged.

WrestleMania's replay coverage was stabilized without changing its ROM: the
A/B pose fixture starts before AI can interrupt it with a grab; the graphics
stress fixture keeps native energy high so it can exercise the full 1,200-frame
interval with live AI. Its private-RAM guard recognizes the precise custom
name/portrait bridge writes. The full native Standard return through the intro,
title, selection and second match has enough host time while retaining its
emulated-frame bound. Separate real damage, defeat and pin replays still pass.

  Contra, corrected-physics co-op profile, 600 video frames:
    Before volley optimization: 390 game updates
    After: 422 (the full-suite run measured 421)

    The final late-stage 6 replay measured 366/600 updates.

Heavy co-op slowdown remains. In particular, the Rangers Fire co-op fixture is
still about 31 game updates per second. Reducing redundant work improves it
but does not establish constant 60-Hz updates on the NES.

Source comparisons isolate ordinary movement on controlled terrain. Native
collisions, water, object support and scripted actions retain host behavior.
The tests do not certify every SMB1 collision quirk, every campaign situation,
a full unassisted campaign clear, or physical NES timing.
